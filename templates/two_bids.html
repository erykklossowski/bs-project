🔽 Download PSE Data

Automatically download aFRR-down capacity prices, procurement volumes, and market predictors from PSE API
Data Sources:
• aFRR-down Prices: cmbu-tu endpoint (afrr_d field) - Primary underlying for Black-Scholes
• Procurement Volumes: zmb endpoint (zmb_afrrd field) - Actual MW for budget constraints
• Market Predictors: price-cost endpoint (CEN, COR, SK) - For μₜ drift modeling
💼 Portfolio Configuration
💰 Capacity Leg (K_cap - Option Premium)
Capacity Leg: Pay-as-bid option premium for readiness/availability. You bid K_cap and receive K_cap × acceptance_probability × portfolio_MW for each interval you're accepted.
⚡ Energy Leg (K_energy - Strike Price)
Energy Leg: Separate energy merit order participation. When activated (probability θ), you receive payment based on the selected pay rule. Negative K_energy means you're willing to pay to provide down-regulation.
🧮 Model Configuration
💨 Wind BSP Constraints
Wind BSP Features: Deliverable capacity = min(portfolio_MW, seasonal_CF × technical_availability × portfolio_MW). Seasonal patterns reflect Polish wind conditions with CVaR risk assessment using block bootstrap methodology.
🔍 Two-Bid Model Architecture:
• Capacity Revenue: K_cap × acceptance_prob(K_cap) × portfolio_MW × 0.25h
• Energy Revenue: [if accepted] × activation_prob(θ) × energy_payoff × portfolio_MW
• Total Revenue: Capacity + Energy legs combined per interval
• Data Sources: PSE aFRR prices, volumes, and CEB balancing energy prices
